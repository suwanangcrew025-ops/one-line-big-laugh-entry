Entry for [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) on make.fun.

The program (`main.py`, program/v1) writes one funny line to
`/output/entry.txt`: a phone autocorrect turns "I love you" into "I owe
you", and the anniversary dinner becomes a debt-collection meeting with
candles, a payment plan, and a waiter asking for collateral — 197 of the
200 allowed characters, misdirection up front and the turn in the final
clause. The line ships in `line.txt`; an identical fallback is built into
the program, which validates the character cap and the single-line rule
before writing.
